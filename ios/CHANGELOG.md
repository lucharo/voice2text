# v2t for iPhone and iPad: changes

What changed for people using the app and keyboard, newest first. The Mac app has its own
[CHANGELOG](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Unreleased

- Keyboard settings and Debugging are sections of the app's home page.
- Debugging shows in TestFlight builds only.
- History cards are one height; tap one to read all of it.
- Copy a dictation, or export all of History from its share button.
- A swipe begun with a stray letter in the field no longer picks an odd word.

## 1.1

Waiting for App Review: build 127 was submitted on 12 November 2026. TestFlight builds 124 to 127,
12 November 2026.

- Fewer wrong autocorrections, and a quicker keyboard.
- Words you stretch on purpose, like "soooo", stay as you typed them.
- The suggestion bar always shows the word a space will put in.
- The bar tells you when a word you keep joins your dictionary.
- You can cancel a dictation while it transcribes; turning the microphone off stops it.
- A new About v2t page opens on the first launch.
- iPhone 11, iPhone 12 and iPhone SE (2nd generation) have no dictation yet; typing works.
- Keys go back to letters when an app empties the field.
- Debugging has a Typing exercise: ten sentences to copy with the keyboard.

## 1.0.1

On the App Store: build 123, submitted on 10 November 2026. TestFlight build 123, 10 November 2026.

- While it transcribes, the keyboard counts the seconds, then says how long it took.
- With Apple Intelligence, long dictations are ready sooner: cleanup starts while you talk.
- An opt-in log of your typing, to find where the keyboard goes wrong (Debugging).

## 1.0

On the App Store: submitted on 9 November 2026. TestFlight builds 1 to 3 (5 November 2026) and
102 to 122 (6 to 8 November 2026); which of them Apple reviewed is not recorded.

- A typing keyboard for iPhone and iPad with v2t dictation built in.
- Speech is transcribed on the device; audio never leaves it.
- Swipe typing, autocorrect and suggestions, in English and Spanish.
- Emoji, with search, suggestions and a skin tone per person.
- After the first dictation, the mic records without leaving the app you type in.
- Music keeps playing as usual while the microphone is on.
- The speech model downloads only when you tap Download; Storage deletes it.
- Words you keep on the keyboard join your v2t dictionary.
